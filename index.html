Admission

Entry requirements, plainly stated.

All applicants must hold a National Senior Certificate certified by Umalusi, or an equivalent foreign qualification converted by SAQA. Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) pathways are available.

Degree programmes

BComp  ·  BIT  ·  BIT Part-Time

  • NSC endorsed for Degree studies
  • Minimum 50% in English (Home Language or First Additional Language)
  • Minimum 50% in pure Mathematics
    Or completion of the Belgium Campus Mathematics Bridging Course at 50%+

Students of all ages are accepted. Belgium Campus also accepts applications through Recognition of Prior Learning.

Diploma programmes

Diploma in IT  ·  Diploma for Deaf Students

  • NSC endorsed for Diploma studies
  • Students of all ages are accepted
  • Recognition of Prior Learning pathways available
  • Diploma for Deaf Students additionally requires:
    50%+ on the Belgium Campus Aptitude Test  ·  Audiogram no older than 6 months

In 2023, Belgium Campus became the first institution in South Africa to graduate six Deaf students with Bachelor of IT degrees.
